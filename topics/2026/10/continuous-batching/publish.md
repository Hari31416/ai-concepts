## Title

Why static LLM batching wastes 33% of GPU compute (and the fix)

## Description

Static batching locks LLM requests together until the slowest sequence finishes, leaving short slots idle and blocking queued requests. Continuous batching schedules at every token step: finished sequences are evicted immediately and waiting requests fill their slots with zero bubbles.

Illustrative 5-request example (B = 3); the 2x - 4x throughput figure is from Orca (Yu et al., 2022).
