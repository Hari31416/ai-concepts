## Title

Why LLMs wait on memory (and how speculative decoding fixes it)

## Description

Generating text one token at a time forces a 70B model to load ~140 GB of weights from GPU memory for every single token, leaving tensor cores mostly idle. Speculative decoding uses a small draft model to guess the next few tokens, then lets the large target model verify all of them in one parallel forward pass with zero quality loss.

The 70B (~140 GB FP16) example and token counts are illustrative; the 70-85% acceptance rate and 2x-3x speedup figures come from Leviathan et al. (2023) and Chen et al. (2023).
